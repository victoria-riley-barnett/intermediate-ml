These are the <Ref to="queries-keys-values">queries, keys and values</Ref> of four positions, each row a projection of one position.

<Atom name="attention-qkv" />

Every query is compared with every key. Row $i$ of $Q$ against column $j$ of $K^\top$ gives the score $s_{ij}$, so four positions give sixteen scores.

<Atom name="attention-scores">

**`S = QKᵀ`.** The tinted row and column make the outlined score; the line below writes that one out.

</Atom>

The scores are <Ref to="scaled-scores">divided by the square root of the width</Ref>, here $\sqrt 4 = 2$.

<Atom name="attention-scale" />

A <Ref to="softmax">softmax</Ref> along each row turns the scores into shares.

<Atom name="attention-weights">

**`A = softmax(S/√d)`.** Every row sums to 1, and each of the four queries puts its largest share on a different key.

</Atom>

Each output row is its row of shares applied to the values: a weighted average, so no output entry can leave the range of the values it mixes.

<Atom name="attention-output">

**`Y = AV`.** Row 1 of `Y` is the tinted row of `A` applied to the four rows of `V`.

</Atom>
